Health knowledge base

Simple habits that save lives

Regular awareness, screening, and early action remain the strongest tools for prevention and survival.

01

Monthly self-checks

Become familiar with your normal breast tissue so changes like lumps, dimpling, or discharge are noticed early.

02

Clinical breast exams

Routine check-ups with trained providers help identify issues before symptoms become obvious.

03

Screening mammography

Low-dose imaging can detect tumors up to two years before they are physically felt, especially from age 40 onward.
